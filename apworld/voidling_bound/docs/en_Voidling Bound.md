# Voidling Bound

## What does randomization do to this game?

Access to Solum, Aulis and the Abyss is locked behind items. Completing missions, collecting every golden egg
or bonus mutagen in a mission, ranking up as a Wrangler and reaching new Abyss depths send checks, and the items
you get back are planet unlocks, DNA Catalyzers, golden and species eggs, research points and mutagens.

Missions on a planet you haven't unlocked yet show a Wrangler level requirement of 99. The real requirement
comes back as soon as the planet's access item arrives.

Depending on the Collection Checks option, every Voidling variant you unlock (294, about 30 per species), or every gene
you unlock (1,042) are checks too. These only ever hold filler, so a variant or gene that refuses to hatch
never blocks anyone.

## What is the goal?

Depending on your options, either beat End to the Unknown or reach a set depth in the Abyss.

## What does another world's item look like in Voidling Bound?

Items are sent and received in the background. The Voidling Bound Client shows what you found and received.
